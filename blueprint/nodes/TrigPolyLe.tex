\begin{definition}[Real trigonometric polynomial of degree at most $m$]
Let $m\in\mathbb{N}$. A function $T:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ is a \emph{real
trigonometric polynomial of degree at most $m$} if there exist two real
sequences $a:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R}$ and $b:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R}$ such that
\[
  T(t)=\sum_{k=0}^{m}\bigl(a_k\cos(kt)+b_k\sin(kt)\bigr)
  \qquad\text{for every }t\in\mathbb{R}.
\]
We write $\operatorname{TrigPolyLe}(m,T)$ for this property. Note that the
coefficient sequences are indexed by all of $\mathbb{N}$, but only the values
$a_0,\dots,a_m$ and $b_0,\dots,b_m$ enter the sum.
\end{definition}
